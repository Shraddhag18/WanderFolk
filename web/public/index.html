Lake Tahoe · 3 days · the plan
Day 1, Saturday
Leave Oakland at 9:00 and stop for lunch in Auburn. Check in to the cabin at 3:00 PM, under 3 hours of driving in all.
Walk the Emerald Bay shore trail, 2 miles, then dinner at a vegetarian-friendly taqueria in South Lake Tahoe.
Day 2, Sunday
A slow morning, first plans at 10:00. The Rubicon Trail to Vikingsholm, 4.5 miles.
BBQ night at the cabin: free and low-cost stops all afternoon.
Day 3, Monday
Breakfast by the lake, pack up by 11:00, home before dark.
